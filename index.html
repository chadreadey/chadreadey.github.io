Featured Projects

AI Financial Analyst

Type a ticker and six specialist AI analysts, each modeled on a different firm's method, run in parallel over SEC filings, fundamentals, sentiment, and macro data. A synthesis agent resolves their contradictions into one investment brief. Underneath sits an XGBoost signal stack validated with combinatorial purged cross-validation.

Outcome × study design

Evidence Gap Mapper private

Give it a drug. It retrieves and classifies the published evidence into an outcome-by-study-design matrix and surfaces the empty cells, the research gaps. Every gap claim is derived from classified records, never asserted, and the tool ships with a measured error rate.

Telegram · n8n · Obsidian

Brain private

A personal intelligence platform. A Telegram bot is the front end, n8n handles orchestration, and an Obsidian vault is long-term memory. Agents file research, email digests, paper-trading logs, and cross-domain syntheses into it on a schedule.

Macro Paper Book private

A multi-asset paper trading book that mirrors a macro newsletter's trades through Alpaca, then layers its own discovery agents on top to test whether the ideas actually hold up out of sample.
